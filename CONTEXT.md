# Relationship Memory

A private record of the people Josué knows, their shared history, and the relationships he wants to maintain deliberately.

## Language

**Person**: Someone represented in the owner's network, including someone whose contact details are not yet known. A person may belong to several circles.
_Avoid_: Lead, prospect

**Circle**: An intentional grouping of people, such as Dartmouth mentors or Schwarzman classmates. Membership does not imply a particular relationship type or contact frequency.

**Cohort**: A circle whose membership belongs to a particular program and class year. A directory entry does not imply that the owner has met the person.

**Interaction**: A dated or approximately dated exchange involving the owner and one or more people, such as a conversation, email, call, or text.

**Meaningful contact**: An interaction the owner considers sufficient to restart a relationship's check-in interval. Importing a profile, writing a private note, and preparing a message do not count.

**Memory**: Context about a relationship preserved from the owner's notes or an identified source. Confirmed recollections and uncertain interpretations remain distinguishable.

**Interview**: A conversation in which the assistant helps the owner recall people and interactions, then proposes memories and actions for review.

**Keep-in-touch plan**: The owner's chosen cadence, preferred channel, and next check-in for a person. It can be paused independently of circle membership.
_Avoid_: Importance score, mentor score

**Check-in**: A planned opportunity to reconnect, with an explanation of why it is due. Completing one requires recording actual contact or an explicit decision to skip it.

**Open loop**: An unfinished commitment, question, or follow-up arising from an interaction. Its deadline is independent of the ordinary keep-in-touch cadence.

**Personal update**: Something happening in the owner's life that they may choose to share with particular people or circles.

**Conversation preferences**: The owner's sense of which topics, formats, and kinds of exchange fit a particular relationship. These guide suggestions without restricting a person to a professional category.

**Draft**: A proposed message that the owner can edit and send. Its existence is not evidence that contact occurred.

**Mentor review**: Deciding whether someone is a mentor. This is distinct from deciding when to contact them.
